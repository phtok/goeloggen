Return and renewal

Interlibrary loan and reservations
It is possible to reserve books in advance or to obtain them by interlibrary loan. To do so, please provide us with a list in written form of the titles you want. Orders received before Wednesday noon will be ready for pick-up the following Friday during our opening hours. Reservations will be held for you for two weeks. Interlibrary loans may take up to two weeks.
